Why this experiment

Notes from the worktable

A succulent's rosette is a rose that never needs watering — that was the whole thought that started this set. The first attempts used the rose templates trimmed down, which taught the useful lesson that succulents are geometry where roses are gesture: their leaves repeat in spirals that can be planned, not shaped wet.

What's being learned: which succulents survive the translation to flat paper (echeveria yes, string-of-pearls so far a stubborn no), and how much curl a template can carry before it needs a bone folder. What's next: printing tests on heavier paper, then a round of paper pots. The finished ones will live on the studio windowsill, next to the real ones — a fair contest, since the paper ones are ahead on watering.
