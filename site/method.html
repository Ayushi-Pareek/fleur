Learn the Techniques · The Fleur Method

The real technique, taught

The methods behind the pieces in the collection — the same shaping, veining, and hand-colouring in fine crepe over wire that goes into the client and commission pieces — taught by Ayushi. This is the long-term craft, and it says so plainly: a sculpture can take the studio two weeks, and learning to make one takes weeks of practice. That is what makes it worth learning.

What the method covers

Shaping

Petals shaped wet, over a bone folder edge, so the ruffle sets instead of being creased in. The posture of the flower — the droop, the reach, the unfurl — is decided here.

Veining

The leaf is the tell. Fine veining gives paper the tension of a living surface, and it is the slowest, quietest skill the studio teaches.

Hand-colouring

Every petal is built white, then dyed in dilute layers once its shape is decided — so the gradient belongs to the piece rather than sitting on it.
