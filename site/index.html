Botanical Paper Sculpture

Fresh flowers, without the wilting.

Fleur is the studio of botanical artist Ayushi. Each piece is an original paper sculpture of a living plant — cut, shaped, and painted by hand, and made to be kept.

Why Botanical Paper Sculpture

Made to last

No water, no wilting, no weekly replacements. With basic care, a paper sculpture holds its colour and shape for years — often decades.

In season, all year

Roses in February. Hellebores in August. If a flower belongs in your room, it doesn't have to be in season.

One of a kind

Every leaf is cut and shaped by hand from the studio's own studies of the plant, so no two pieces are identical. Each one is signed and dated by the artist.

The easier bouquet

Nothing to order, trim, or throw away at the end of the week. Fresh arrangements are a repeat purchase. This is a single one.

From the Studio

The Collection

Works are released in small numbers as each is finished — grouped by the plant under study, and shown here while they last. Commissions

A memory, made to endure

The peonies from your wedding bouquet, rebuilt so they last. A bouquet you've always pictured but never found, designed from scratch in paper.

Ayushi works as a paper florist: recreating arrangements that exist, designing ones that don't yet, and taking a small number of private commissions each year. Interior projects welcome.
