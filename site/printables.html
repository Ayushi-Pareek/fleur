Print & Make · the studio's paper experiments

Make something this afternoon

This is the artist's wind-down practice — the quick, satisfying side of a life spent with paper. Print at home, cut, assemble, and end the day with a finished thing in your hands: flowers and succulents, cards and journals, small whimsical pieces for around the house. Everything on this page is an experiment — the flowers included — and none of it is precious. Most pieces take two or three hours from printer to shelf.

What you'll need

One kit covers the whole shelf — flowers, plants, cards, journals. It's probably in your desk drawer already.

A home printer

Any inkjet or laser. Everything here is drawn to print cleanly in plain black on white — the colouring is part of the making.

A4 printer paper

Around 100 gsm — ordinary printer paper. Heavier art paper holds a better curve if you have it, but it isn't required.

Scissors and glue

A craft knife is nicer for the fine edges, but scissors do the job. Any paper glue. That's the whole kit.
