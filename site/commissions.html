Commissions

A memory, made to endure

The peonies from your wedding bouquet, rebuilt so they last. A bouquet you've always pictured but never found, designed from scratch in paper. A specimen for a room you've never quite finished.

Ayushi works as a paper florist: she can recreate an arrangement that exists — so it outlives the occasion — or design one that doesn't yet, proposing the stems, shapes, and colour with you. A small number of private commissions are taken each year; interior projects welcome.

i.

The conversation

A name, an email, and a rough scale — that's all it takes to begin. The studio replies within a few days, and everything else (the plant, its story, the occasion) is talked through over email.

ii.

The study

You don't need to send the plant — one picture is enough, and nothing needs to be posted. Work can also begin from an idea alone: a bouquet you've pictured but never found, six tulips and four hydrangeas, a colour fresh flowers can't hold. The studio proposes stems, arrangements, and colour schemes, then returns a sketch, a scale, and a quote before anything is made.

iii.

The making

Your piece is built petal by petal over two to six weeks, depending on scale. You see photographs as it comes together.

iv.

The arrival

Signed, dated, packed in a made-to-fit box, and shipped worldwide — tracked from the studio door to yours. Within India, larger pieces can be delivered and placed in the room.
