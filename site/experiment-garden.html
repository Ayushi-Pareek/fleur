Why this experiment

Notes from the worktable

The question behind the set: if you could only grow three paper stems on a windowsill, which three would you want? Candidates are being tested at the worktable through the winter — something tall and airy, something round and full, something in between — on the theory that a garden is an argument between heights.

What's being learned: a set is harder than a single flower, because every stem has to look well next to the other two, and colour has to be shared across the whole download. What's next: a shortlist, then print tests. The finished set will hold the studio's own windowsill first, in a jam jar, before it's allowed out.
