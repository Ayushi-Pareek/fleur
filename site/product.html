Rose No. 2 — Dark Stem

$310

A study in umber and pine, worked from a deep-stemmed garden rose as the bloom began to nod. The petals take hand-mixed colour in layers — the darker the rose, the more layers it asks for.

One of a kind — no two are ever identical. Pieces are made in small numbers; when a batch sells out, the studio makes more in its own time. A specific study may also be commissioned.

  • PlantRosa — garden rose, studied and photographed at the worktable
  • MaterialsWire armature, fine crepe paper, hand-mixed colour, aged terracotta pot
  • DimensionsApprox. 46 cm tall, as the living stem would stand
  • EditionOne of a kind — no two identical; signed and dated by the artist
  • CareDust with cool air; keep from direct sun and damp
  • ShippingWorldwide, tracked, from the studio · 3–5 days to dispatch

Duties and taxes, where they apply, are set by your country and payable on delivery. The piece travels packed in a made-to-fit box, the stem secured as it would stand.
