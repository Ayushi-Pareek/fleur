Why this experiment

Notes from the worktable

This one started as a question about movement: how does flat paper become something that opens? An afternoon was spent folding V-folds and box folds out of ordinary printer paper, and somewhere between the third and fourth attempt the folds turned into petals — a card that opens into a small bouquet felt inevitable after that.

What was learned: a petal's curve is mostly a fold problem, and plain paper is a better teacher than expensive paper. What's next: more occasions — a version that stands on a shelf as a small paper garden, and a birthday card with a single stem that keeps unfolding. The prototypes live on the studio shelf until they're given away, which is the point of a card.
