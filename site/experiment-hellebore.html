Why this experiment

Notes from the worktable

The hellebore earned its place the way winter flowers do: by showing up when nothing else will. February's studies are the source — the nodding cups photographed from below, the way the petals hold a soft green long after they've opened — and the pattern is the plain-paper version of that study, curves included.

What's being learned: the nodding head is a stem problem, not a petal problem — the curve has to be built into the wire of the template, or printed as a fold. What's next: petal-count tests between now and the opening, and a decision on how much green to leave in the cup. Come February it goes on the studio's own kitchen table, which is where the winter flowers live.
